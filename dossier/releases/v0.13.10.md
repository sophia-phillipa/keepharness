# 0.13.10 — Round-ten authority, recovery and accessibility repairs

## Behavior

- Streaming file/workspace uploads revalidate the request credential, owner, project and upload eligibility after waits. Media uploads also recheck the selected provider/model. Rejected uploads leave no stored record or temporary source/archive.
- Directory browsing and Codex/Claude usage reads revalidate credentials before delivery; browsing also checks that shared-project registration remains enabled. Internal revalidation does not consume another request-rate allowance.
- Ordinary and Maestro-plan gates retain sanitized public specifications and choices independently of raw execution data. Known secret-bearing option identifiers are rejected instead of creating an unusable public choice.
- Failed, interrupted and cancelled runs retry transient SQLite terminal writes while retaining the result and scheduler ownership. Inference is never retried by this storage path. Shutdown remains responsive and leaves uncommitted state to startup recovery.
- Accepted Resume/rerun retries use a persisted identity of the caller request, so later source edits or deletion do not hide the accepted child. Changed requests still conflict, and current owner/project authority is checked before reuse.
- Explicit reruns keep consumed publication approvals as resolved historical records while obtaining fresh authorization. Unused approvals still invalidate when their binding changes.
- Frontend/backend Markdown masks close fences only at the opening blockquote depth. Deeper quoted fences do not expose literal resource or reserved-marker examples.
- The mobile Attention glyph remains painted, and its popup anchors to the trigger within the viewport. Resource previews reserve visible option space at native 200% zoom. The editor exposes combobox selection/expanded state and announces empty/loading results.
- Readiness recovery restores the Settings opener or active console control after sustained outages. Queue messages explain provider capacity and other known scheduling reasons in plain language without changing protocol values.
